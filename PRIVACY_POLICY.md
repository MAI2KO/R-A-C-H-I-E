# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **mAI Developments** ("we", "us", "our") processes personal information when operating the Discord applications **R.A.C.H.I.E** and **P.E.G.G.I.E** (together, the "Bots").

For privacy-related requests, contact:

**mAI Developments**  
Email: **marko595@icloud.com**

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through R.A.C.H.I.E and P.E.G.G.I.E.

R.A.C.H.I.E primarily supports Whiteout Survival communities.

P.E.G.G.I.E primarily supports Kingshot communities.

The Bots are unofficial community tools and are not operated by Discord, Century Games, Whiteout Survival, Kingshot, Google, Railway, or OpenAI.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on which features you or your Discord server use, the Bots may process the following information.

### Discord information

- Discord user ID;
- Discord server/guild ID;
- Discord channel ID;
- Discord role information;
- Discord usernames or display names where required for presentation;
- command interactions;
- server configuration; and
- identifiers associated with messages or interactions needed to operate Bot features.

### Game-related information

Where you register a game character or use gift-code features, we may process:

- game Player ID;
- State number for Whiteout Survival;
- Kingdom number for Kingshot;
- registered character/account associations;
- gift-code auto-redemption preferences;
- gift-code redemption results;
- gift-code verification results; and
- limited technical information returned by relevant redemption services.

The Bots do not require your game password.

Do not provide your game password to the Bots.

### Event and booking information

Where event, reminder, or booking features are used, we may process:

- event names;
- event times;
- alliance or group names;
- reminder preferences;
- booking selections;
- in-game names;
- Player IDs where required for a booking feature; and
- related Discord identifiers.

### Gift-code contribution information

If you submit or contribute a gift code, we may process:

- the submitted gift code;
- your Discord user ID;
- the Discord server in which it was submitted;
- verification status; and
- contribution/reward information.

### AI banter information

Where a server administrator enables the AI banter feature, the Bot may process **up to 10 recent messages from the Discord channel in which the feature is active** in order to generate a contextual response.

This may include:

- message content;
- Discord display names or similar contextual information where included by the Bot; and
- conversation context required to generate the response.

The relevant content may be transmitted to **OpenAI** for processing in order to generate the AI response.

Banter responses may include informal language or profanity.

The Bots are not intended to collect sensitive personal information through this feature. Users should avoid posting sensitive personal information in channels where AI banter is enabled.

## 3. How We Use Information

We process information where necessary to provide and operate the Bots, including to:

- respond to Discord commands;
- associate users with registered game characters;
- verify gift codes;
- automatically attempt gift-code redemption;
- track redemption results and avoid duplicate processing;
- deliver redemption notifications;
- operate event schedules and reminders;
- manage minister or appointment bookings;
- create weekly event summaries;
- manage server configuration;
- provide administrative diagnostics;
- detect and resolve operational errors;
- prevent abuse and duplicate processing;
- operate contribution or reward features;
- generate AI banter responses where enabled; and
- maintain the security and reliability of the Bots.

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use personal information for third-party advertising.

## 4. Lawful Bases

Where UK data-protection law applies, we rely primarily on the following lawful bases.

### Legitimate interests

We may process information where necessary for our legitimate interests in:

- operating and maintaining the Bots;
- providing requested community-management functionality;
- preventing duplicate or abusive automated activity;
- maintaining security and reliability;
- diagnosing technical faults; and
- improving the operation of Bot features.

We consider these interests against the rights and interests of affected users and aim to limit processing to what is reasonably necessary.

### Contract / requested service

Where you directly choose to register a character, use a command, configure a server, submit a code, or request a Bot function, some processing may be necessary to provide the service you have requested.

### Legal obligation

We may process or retain information where necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal requirement.

Where another lawful basis is required for a specific activity, we will use the basis appropriate to that processing.

## 5. AI Processing and OpenAI

When the AI banter feature is enabled, up to 10 recent messages from the active channel may be transmitted to OpenAI to generate a response.

OpenAI acts as a third-party service provider for this functionality.

Only the amount of conversational context needed for the feature should be transmitted.

AI-generated responses may be inaccurate, unexpected, or contain profanity.

Server administrators should inform their community if they enable AI banter in channels where users may reasonably expect their messages to be processed for this purpose.

## 6. Service Providers and Data Sharing

We may share or transmit information to service providers only where necessary to operate the Bots.

Current providers may include:

### Discord

Discord provides the platform, API, interactions, messages, servers, users, and permissions through which the Bots operate.

### Railway

Railway is used to host Bot services and infrastructure.

### PostgreSQL

PostgreSQL databases are used to store operational data such as:

- registered player accounts;
- server configuration;
- gift-code status;
- redemption history;
- event configuration; and
- other persistent Bot state.

### Google

Google Apps Script and Google Sheets are currently used for certain legacy or booking-related functionality.

Relevant booking or configuration information may therefore be transmitted to and stored using Google services.

### OpenAI

OpenAI is used for AI-powered banter functionality.

When that feature is enabled, limited recent channel-message context may be transmitted to OpenAI as described above.

### Game-related services

Player IDs, State/Kingdom information, and gift codes may be transmitted to relevant publicly available game redemption services where required to verify or redeem gift codes.

We do not share personal information with advertisers or sell personal information to third parties.

## 7. International Processing

Some service providers used by the Bots may process information outside the United Kingdom.

Where UK data-protection law applies, we take reasonable steps to use providers and arrangements that provide appropriate protections for personal information.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, including operation, security, troubleshooting, audit, and abuse prevention.

Retention depends on the type of information.

For example:

- active player registrations may be retained while the account remains registered;
- server configuration may be retained while a server continues to use the Bots;
- gift-code and redemption records may be retained where necessary to prevent duplicate redemptions and preserve operational history;
- event and booking information may be retained for the period needed to provide the relevant feature;
- technical logs and diagnostic records may be retained for a limited period where needed to investigate faults or abuse; and
- information may be retained longer where required by law.

Where information is no longer reasonably required, it will be deleted, anonymised, or otherwise removed from active use where practicable.
